About MES

Municipal Engineering Services Limited (MES) is a Nigerian engineering and community development company committed to delivering practical, affordable, and sustainable solutions to infrastructure and housing challenges.

We believe that successful projects are not measured only by the structures built but by the positive impact they create within the communities they serve.

Our approach combines engineering excellence, social responsibility, and long-term sustainability.


Why Choose MES

Professional Experience

Built on decades of practical experience in engineering, public infrastructure, and community development.

Community-Focused Solutions

Quality and Accountability

Sustainable Development

 *SPECIALIZATION PAGE* 

Drainage and Community Infrastructure Specialists

Poor drainage is one of the most overlooked causes of infrastructure failure in many Nigerian communities.

MES provides practical drainage solutions designed to protect roads, properties, and public infrastructure.

Our services include:

- Drainage assessment
- Drainage design
- In-situ concrete drainage construction
- Culvert construction
- Roadside drainage improvement
- Community drainage planning

Our Principle

Every Successful Road Begins with Proper Drainage.
